Camp Log

— where I slept, in my own words —

Every place Codiwomple parked for the night — the campground or the pullout, how many nights, what I saw, what I ate, and whether I'd go back. I keep the log in StayTonight, the app I built for exactly this, and the entries I mark public land here the moment I publish them.

Turning the pages…

Want the map? Every stay is a pin on the journal map, red line through them in the order I traveled. And when a stay lines up with a post in my FindPenguins journal, that post appears right under the entry — photos, penguin and all.

If one of these places is on your way and you want the site number, the road in, or the honest verdict, drop me a line — I've usually got more to say than fits on the page.

See you down the road.
— Jersey Mark
